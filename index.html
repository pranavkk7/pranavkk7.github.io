Main project

Unique Fitness Gym

A front-desk app for a real gym in Pinarayi, Kerala, built in Flutter for the owner's Android phone and tablet. It runs the day at the desk and talks directly to the gym's Face ID door.

  • Flutter and DartOffline-first, data kept on the device with Hive
  • Android, iPhone, tabletPlus a web demo you can try
  • 99 automated testsRun by CI on every push
  • eSSL / ZKTecoThe door's network protocol, written from scratch
01

A morning at the desk

One screen answers the owner's first questions: who came in, what was collected, whose plan ends this week. The door log draws a tick for every entry since 5 AM.

02

Face ID at the door

The app speaks the eSSL terminal's own network protocol. It enrols members' faces, pulls check-ins every few minutes and locks out expired plans at the door, with a first-time setup guide for the owner.

03

An admission in a minute

Pick a plan, see the saving against monthly, take cash, UPI or card, and share a PDF receipt. Partial payments become dues the app remembers.

04

Reminders, one tap each

Expiring plans, dues, birthdays and missed workouts are listed every morning. WhatsApp opens with each message written; the desk only taps send.

05

The owner's numbers

Income against a monthly target, profit after expenses, how members pay and the busy hours, behind an owner PIN so the desk sees only what it needs.

06

Three looks, one app

The owner picks the look: the original dark red design, a calm light one, or its dark mode. Every feature works in all three, and the choice is saved on the device.
